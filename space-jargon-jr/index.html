Space Jargon Jr

A colorful spelling game for kids aged 4 to 10. Spell your way through the stars.

Swipe across a wheel of letters to spell each word and fill the mission. Every word fills your rocket, and every mission cleared lights up the Star Map. Seven planets, one for each age, take young spellers from cat and dog all the way to gravity and science.

Coming soon on Google Play Paid app on Android. No ads and no in-app purchases.

What's on board

  • Seven planets, ages 4 to 1056 missions of hand-picked words, from three-letter words to science and story words.
  • Hear every wordWords and meanings are read aloud with the phone's own offline voice.
  • Learn while spellingEach word shows its meaning and a pattern tip, like how ee makes the sound in tree.
  • Never stuckPeek and Mix are always free. No timers and no lives.
  • Spell FlightExtra practice: see a picture or a meaning, then spell the word.
  • Word BookEvery word your child finds is kept, ready to practice again.
  • Star StoreStars earned in play buy stickers, wheel colors and sky scenes.
  • Grown-up cornerPick the starting age and reading voice, behind a simple grown-up check.

A look around

One price, nothing more

Space Jargon Jr is a paid app with no ads and no in-app purchases. Stars are earned by playing, never bought, and there are no links out of the game.

It works fully offline. Progress stays on the device, and there are no accounts. The privacy policy has the details.
