Space Jargon Jr — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Overview

Space Jargon Jr is a spelling game for Android, made for children aged 4 to 10. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is stored, and what is shared.

Data We Access

None. The app asks for no permissions.

Data Stored on Your Device

The only data stored on your device is game progress: missions cleared, words found, stars, Star Store items and grown-up settings such as the starting age and reading voice. It stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone). We do not use analytics or tracking. We do not display ads. We do not sell in-app purchases. We do not share data with third parties. We do not operate backend servers. The game has no internet access and runs fully offline.

Read-aloud Voice

Words and meanings are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine already installed on the device. Nothing is recorded, and no audio or text is sent anywhere.

Children's Privacy

Space Jargon Jr is designed for children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no chat and no links out of the game.

Your Rights

You can erase all progress with Erase progress in the Grown-up corner, and uninstall the app to remove all local data.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the developer email on the game's Google Play page once released.

Space Jargon Jr is made by Thendar Games.
