Word Jungle

A colorful word-connect game. You wake in a strange jungle and spell your way home.

Swipe a wheel of letters to fill every word on the board. Each cleared level pours gas into your plane, and a full tank flies you to the next village. Sixteen villages spiral inward on the journey map, and the ? at the center is Home.

Get it on Google Play Free on Android.

What's in the jungle

  • No timers, no livesThe main journey goes at your pace.
  • SightingsFind real words that aren't on the board to earn seeds and grow your streak.
  • Hints when you're stuckWhisper, Scout and Lantern. Shuffle is always free.
  • The MarketBoosts, mystery nests, camp themes, plane paints and upgrades.
  • Daily puzzlesDawn Call and the Lost Tongue glyph puzzle, plus optional timed modes.
  • Endless playThe endless path after Home, and Deeper Jungle to start the journey again, harder.
  • AccessibilityColorblind-safe colors, larger text and reduced flash.
  • English and FrenchPlay with the interface in either language.

A look around

Free to play

Word Jungle is free, with optional ads. Remove ads in the Market for $0.99 a month or $4.99 forever. Rewarded ads, which give you free hints and seeds, are always your choice.

Your progress stays on your device, and there are no accounts. The privacy policy explains how ads and purchases work.
