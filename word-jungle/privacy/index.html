Word Jungle

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the Word Jungle app ("the game") handles, and why. Word Jungle is made by Thendar ("I", "me").

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. I don't collect, see or store your personal information.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, such as your advertising ID, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. I never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, seeds, unlocked items and your "Remove ads" status are saved in the app's local storage on your device. They are never sent to me.

  • Backup codes: Settings can create a backup code of your progress. It is only shared if you choose to copy or send it yourself.
  • Deleting it: Settings → Reset progress erases your progress. Uninstalling the game removes everything it stored.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC:

  • Rewarded ads, which you choose to watch. For example, a free hint or bonus seeds.
  • Occasional full-screen ads between villages.

To serve, limit, measure and protect ads against fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version and app version;
  • how you interact with ads;
  • diagnostic and crash data related to ads.

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent and your choices

  • Consent first: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a region with similar rules (including some US states), the game shows Google's consent form before any ads load. You can choose whether ads may be personalized.
  • Changing your choice: open Settings → Help → Privacy options in the game. This option appears where the law requires it.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it at any time in your device's settings. On most Android devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The game offers an optional "Remove ads" purchase, either as a monthly subscription or a one-time purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The game only receives confirmation from Google Play of what your account owns, so that it can turn ads off. I do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Updates

The game asks the Google Play Store app on your device whether a newer version is available, so it can offer to update. This check is handled by Google Play.

Permissions

The game uses these Android permissions:

  • Vibration: for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Internet and network state: used only to load ads and to talk to Google Play. The word puzzles themselves work offline.
  • Advertising ID and ad-measurement permissions: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.
  • Billing: used by Google Play to process purchases.

Children

Word Jungle is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact me and I will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use.

  • Information Google collects through ads or Google Play: you can exercise these rights through Google, at Google's privacy controls.
  • Information the game stores on your device: you are always in control of it, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the developer email on the game's Google Play page.
