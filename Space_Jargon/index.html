A zen sci-fi word-connect game. You are a long-range probe drifting between quiet sectors of space.

Swipe across a wheel of letters to spell words and fill every slot. Each cleared level fuels your rocket, and a full tank warps you to the next sector. Sixteen sectors spiral inward on the Star Map, and the ? at the center opens the endless Drift.
