Space Jargon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Overview

Space Jargon is a word puzzle Android app with a sci-fi theme. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is stored, and what is shared.

Data We Access

None

Data Stored on Your Device

None

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone). We do not use analytics or tracking. We do not display ads. We do not share data with third parties. We do not operate backend servers. The game runs fully offline.

Notifications

The app can send local notifications for events. These are generated entirely on your device. No notification data is sent externally. You can disable all notifications in Settings.

Network Communication

All network traffic uses TLS 1.2+ encryption.

Children's Privacy

This app is available for all ages.

Your Rights

You can reset all cached data from Settings, and uninstall the app to remove all local data.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/thendar-git/Space_Jargon

Space Jargon is made by Thendar Games.
